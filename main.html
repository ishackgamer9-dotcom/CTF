Incident report: The fall of Earth

The universe has witnessed the rise and fall of countless civilizations. Empires have crumbled, entire worlds have vanished, and legends have faded into the silence of space. Yet nothing could have prepared Earth for the arrival of Beerus, the God of Destruction.

In another telling of this story, Beerus found amusement in the customs of mortals and eventually departed without destroying the planet. But this is not that story.

This time, there was no mercy.

The skies trembled as Beerus descended upon Earth. The Z-Fighters gathered to protect their home, knowing that the fate of every living soul rested upon a battle they might not survive. At the center stood Son Goku: a warrior who had faced tyrants, monsters, and enemies whose strength once seemed impossible to overcome.

Goku attacked with everything he possessed, but the God of Destruction answered with terrifying ease. The difference between them was not merely a gap in strength. It was the distance between a mortal warrior and a being who held the power to erase entire worlds.

Still, Goku refused to surrender. He thought of his family, his friends, and the people who trusted him to protect their home. He had never fought because he believed he could not lose. He fought because some things were worth protecting even when victory seemed impossible.

The final clash illuminated the heavens. Energy tore through the atmosphere, the earth shook beneath their feet, and the shockwaves echoed across the planet. Goku gave everything: every ounce of strength, every remaining breath, every fragment of determination that had carried him through a lifetime of battles.

But Beerus remained standing.

The defender of Earth had fallen, and the God of Destruction showed no mercy. Silence descended upon the battlefield. Earth's greatest warrior had lost, and the being responsible possessed the power to erase everything that remained.

A clue survived

During his final moments, Goku attempted to preserve something beyond his own survival. It was not a weapon or a technique that could be learned through ordinary training. It was a clue, a fragment of information connected to an ancient Saiyan legend.

Capsule Corporation's recovery bots eventually discovered a damaged note attributed to Goku. Most of it was lost, but eleven characters survived. The scientists believe the characters are not the message itself, but a path leading to a surviving record that may reveal the identity of the one warrior capable of challenging Beerus.

“With his final breath, Goku left us something that may yet lead to the end of Beerus. Find the transmission, follow the surviving connections, and uncover the power that could save our world.”
